# Official Announcement: Spring 2026 Course Registration

The Office of the Registrar provided VinUniversity students with detailed information about class schedule release and course registration for the Spring 2026 semester.

## Spring 2026 Class Schedule Release

The Spring 2026 class schedule release was December 15 to December 17, 2025.

Students were advised to check available sections for each course and prepare a preliminary course list before registration.

## Course Registration Opens

The SIS course registration portal opened at 2:00 PM on December 18, 2025.

Students could register for courses according to individual study plans, select sections that fit their schedules, and complete registration for required and elective courses.

Course Registration Portal (SIS): https://sis.vinuni.edu.vn/cmcportal/

## Course Retake Registration

Students who needed to retake a previously failed course were required to submit a Course Retake Registration Form for the Office of the Registrar to review and process.

Course retake requests had to be submitted within the official registration period. Approval was subject to academic regulations, course availability, and prerequisite conditions. Late submissions might not be considered.

## Note for MD Students

For students in the Doctor of Medicine program, the Office of the Registrar pre-registered all required major and professional courses according to the MD curriculum.

MD students were not required to register for major courses pre-arranged by REG, but they were responsible for independently registering VinCore courses via SIS based on individual study plans.

Students were encouraged to review study plans and curriculum requirements before registration, confirm prerequisites, prepare a tentative course list, and monitor official announcements from the Office of the Registrar.
