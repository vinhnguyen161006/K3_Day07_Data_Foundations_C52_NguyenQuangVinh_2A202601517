# Class Schedule and Course Registration

Welcome to the course registration hub. This page guides students through how to prepare, register, and manage courses at VinUniversity.

## Key Steps to Follow

1. Review your study plan.
2. Consult the curriculum framework and understand degree structure, course requirements, and prerequisites.
3. Plan a tentative schedule before registration opens.
4. Register on SIS by logging in to the Student Information System and registering for courses.
5. Follow the add/drop timeline. During the first few days of the semester, students can make changes directly in SIS.
6. Submit special requests. For retakes, audits, or withdrawals, contact the Registrar's Office via email.

## SIS Course Registration Flow

Students should use the SIS course registration portal when this general guide applies.

1. Log in to SIS with a VinUni email and password.
2. Go to "Academics" > "Course Registration", check the correct term, and click "Register" if the status is "Open".
3. Search for courses by course code or name. Students can filter by College, semester part, or time slot.
4. Add courses to the cart and register. Click "Add", then "Register" to finalize.
5. Check that the course status is "Registered", not "Selected". "Selected" means that the course has not been registered.
6. Confirm registered courses under "Your Class Schedule".

## Troubleshooting and Common Issues

Common registration issues include system errors, course visibility problems, unmet prerequisites, waitlists, course conflicts, full courses, maximum credit overload requests, and system access or technical errors.

Some courses have limited seats. Once a course is full, students cannot register unless a spot opens. No waitlist function is currently available in SIS, so students are advised to check SIS regularly for open slots.

SIS automatically blocks registration if prerequisites are not met. If students believe they are eligible but cannot register, they should contact the Registrar's Office.

SIS will not allow registration for overlapping courses.
