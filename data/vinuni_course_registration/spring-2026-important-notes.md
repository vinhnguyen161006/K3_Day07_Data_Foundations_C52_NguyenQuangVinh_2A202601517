# Important Announcement for Spring 2026 Semester

The Office of University Registrar shared important information and reminders for faculty members, teaching assistants, academic assistants, and students for the Spring 2026 semester.

## Course Schedules

Students are required to confirm their class schedules, including course times and locations, before the first day of instruction.

Students should verify that registered courses on SIS are correctly synchronized and displayed on Canvas.

If a course registered on SIS does not appear on Canvas, students are advised to report the issue promptly to the Office of University Registrar.

## Course Add, Drop, and Withdrawal Policy

Students may add or drop courses within the officially published add/drop period for each semester as stated in the Academic Calendar. Changes made during this period do not appear on the academic transcript.

After the add/drop period ends, dropping a course is considered a course withdrawal.

A "W - Withdrawn" grade is recorded on the academic transcript when the student has officially registered for a course in a semester and subsequently submits a formal withdrawal notification to the University.

The W grade indicates that the student attempted the course but withdrew before completing it for a letter grade.

A course with a W grade is considered one enrollment attempt. If the student later registers for the same course again, it is treated as a repeat enrollment and the student must pay the tuition fee again according to University regulations.

Withdrawal from a course is permitted after the official course drop period and must occur before more than 30% of the course study time has been completed. After this threshold, withdrawal is no longer allowed, and the student is graded based on academic performance.

If a student withdraws before the official drop deadline for the semester, no W grade is recorded on the transcript.

The withdrawal limit policy allows students to withdraw from a maximum of 18 credits over the entire program of study. Once this limit has been reached, students are no longer permitted to withdraw from courses and must remain enrolled. In such cases, instructors assign final grades based on course performance.
