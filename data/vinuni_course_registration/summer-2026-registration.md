# Official Announcement: Summer 2026 Course Registration

The Office of the Registrar provided VinUniversity students with information about class schedule release, course registration, deadlines, and support for the Summer 2026 semester.

## Summer 2026 Class Schedule Release

The Summer 2026 class schedule release date was June 22, 2026. Students were advised to check available course sections and prepare a tentative study plan before registration.

## Summer 2026 Course Registration Period

The Summer 2026 course registration period took place from June 29 to July 4, 2026.

Students were advised to review the list of courses expected to be offered during the Summer 2026 semester, prepare preliminary study plans in advance, and monitor official announcements from REG for registration instructions and timelines.

## Summer 2026 Instruction Begins

Instruction for Summer 2026 began on July 6, 2026. Students were advised to contact instructors or teaching assistants promptly if any issues arose regarding enrollment or attendance.

## Add/Drop Deadline

July 11, 2026 was the final deadline for students to add or drop courses for the Summer 2026 semester.

After this deadline, no additional course adds or drops were permitted. Students were responsible for ensuring that their final registered courses aligned with their study plans and graduation requirements. Students were strongly encouraged to consult with their Academic Advisor before making schedule changes.

## Additional Summer 2026 Dates

The End-of-Semester Course Evaluation period for Summer 2026 was August 17 to August 21, 2026.

The Final Examination Period for Summer 2026 was August 24 to August 28, 2026.

During the class schedule release and registration period, the Office of the Registrar was available to support students.

## Recommendations for Students

Students were encouraged to review their study plan before registration, confirm that all course prerequisites had been satisfied, prepare a tentative course list in advance, and follow official announcements from the Office of the Registrar.
