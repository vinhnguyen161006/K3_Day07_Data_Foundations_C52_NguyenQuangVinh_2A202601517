# Forms and Petitions

This page provides quick access to commonly used forms related to academic procedures, course registration, and student records at VinUniversity.

Students should read instructions carefully before submitting.

## Academic Requests

Course Registration requests for Retake, Audit, or Individual Study do not list a downloadable form on the page. Students submit the request via email to the Registrar's Office.

Course Withdrawal after the add/drop period does not list a downloadable form on the page. Students submit the request via email with the course instructor's approval.

Credit Transfer uses the VinUni Transfer Credit Form and is submitted with supporting documents to [email protected].

Graduation Application and Double Degree requests are submitted by email to the Registrar's Office.

## Program and Study Status Changes

Change of Program, Major, or Minor uses the VinUni Change of Program Major Minor form and is submitted to the Program Director and Registrar.

Leave of Absence uses the VinUni Defer Withdraw Form and is submitted via email with advisor or Program Director approval.

Return to Study uses the VinUni General Petition Form and is emailed to [email protected], with the Academic Advisor and Program Director copied if applicable.

## Where to Get Help

Some forms may be available only upon request. Students who are unsure which form to use should contact the Registrar's Office.

All forms must be submitted using the student's VinUni email address.
