# Launch of the New Student Portal for Summer 2026 Course Registration

Starting from the Summer 2026 semester, all course registrations are officially conducted through the new VinUniDigi Student Portal.

The new portal allows students to search, register, review, and manage courses more efficiently.

## Course Registration Handbook

The Office of the Registrar prepared a Course Registration Handbook with detailed instructions and screenshots.

The handbook includes guidance on registering for courses, understanding class status and common registration issues, reviewing registered classes, dropping registered classes, previewing the timetable, and the final registration checklist.

Students were strongly recommended to review the handbook before the registration slot opened.

## Access the Student Portal

Student Portal: https://one.vinuni.edu.vn/student

Students sign in using their assigned VinUniversity email account and password.

## Class Status

Students should pay attention to class status before registering.

Available means registration is allowed.

Full means no seats are available.

Conflict means the class overlaps with another registered class.

## Prerequisite Requirements

The system automatically checks prerequisite and pre-study requirements. Registration is not permitted if these requirements have not been satisfied.

## Dropping a Course

Students may drop a registered class only while the registration period is open.

To drop a class, students click the Delete icon next to the course and select OK to confirm.

## Final Registration Checklist

Before completing registration, students should ensure that they have:

- Signed in with their VinUni account.
- Selected the correct academic term.
- Verified prerequisite requirements.
- Checked class availability.
- Avoided timetable conflicts.
- Clicked CONFIRM.
- Verified that all courses display the status Registered.
- Previewed the timetable.
