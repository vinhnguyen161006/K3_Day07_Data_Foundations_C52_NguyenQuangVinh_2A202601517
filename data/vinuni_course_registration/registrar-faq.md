# Registrar FAQs

## Academic Programs and Registration

The Registrar's Office manages student records, course registration, academic calendars, graduation review, and official documents.

Students must register for at least 80% of the standard annual credit load to be considered full-time enrollment. The standard annual credit load is usually around 30 credits per year.

Students applying for a double degree submit a request to the Registrar. The process includes credit equivalency review, faculty approval, and Provost confirmation.

Tuition and fee details are provided by the Finance Office. For academic planning, students contact the Registrar if course load affects tuition.

## Credit and Academic Standing

Academic integrity cases from Tier 2 and above are recorded in the Registrar's system.

## Graduation and Degree Conferral

The Registrar retains graduation documents for the legally required duration.

## Contact and Support

For registration support, students can email [email protected] or call extension 9004 during working hours.

The Registrar supports advising logistics and collaborates with Student Affairs to implement the First-Year Faculty Advising Program.

Colleges, Registrar, and relevant offices are responsible for enforcing academic regulations. The Provost may amend policies when needed.

Academic policies are subject to updates based on MOET or internal decisions. The Registrar is responsible for communicating changes.
